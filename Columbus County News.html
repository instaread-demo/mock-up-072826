At Land O’Lakes, the Southeastern Fellowship Golf Association played its weekly tournament on July 21. 29 members participated in the event, which was a Captain’s Choice this week.

First place went to the team of Bradley Butler, Rudy Pait, Elvin Meares, and Chris Smith with a score of 58. Wilber Simmons, David Medlin, Tony Walters, and Rodney Warwick tied for second with Linwood Hedgepeth, Paul Stanley, Terry Blackburn, and Bobby Gooden with a 59 score for both teams.

The SFGA plays a tournament weekly. The next SFGA event will take place on July 28 at Pinecrest Golf Course. Call 910.642.5757 for more information.

Upcoming tournaments at Land O’Lakes are:

July 25-26 Club Championship Tee Times

Aug. 14 Nine and Dine 5 p.m. Shotgun Start

At Vineland Golf at White Lake, Blake Davis. Johnathan Gooden, Stephen Clark, and Taylor King won the weekly Thundering Herd with a score of -7. 12 players participated in the event. The Thundering Herd is held at 6 p.m. every Thursday. Call 910.247.6132 for more information about the Thundering Herd and other events.

At Vineyard Golf Course of Whiteville, every Thursday, they host a Nine Hole Twilight Tournament at 5:30 p.m. Memberships for $75 a month. For more information, call 910.642.3623.
