ZuZu Nashville celebrates first anniversary with debut of elevated sushi program and new culinary offerings

NASHVILLE, Tenn., (July 2026) – ZuZu Nashville is celebrating its first anniversary with an exciting new chapter in its culinary journey. Since officially opening its doors on July 18, 2025, the restaurant has become a sought-after destination for globally inspired cuisine, vibrant nightlife and unforgettable dining experiences in the heart of Nashville.

To commemorate the milestone, ZuZu is unveiling an all-new sushi program featuring premium ingredients, inventive flavors, and shareable dishes designed to enhance the guest experience. The expanded menu showcases signature creations, including the Hamachi Crown, Imperial Roll and innovative Sushi Tacos, alongside premium hand rolls, an expanded chilled seafood selection and new shareable plates that complement the restaurant’s energetic atmosphere.

Further elevating the dining experience, ZuZu has introduced a thoughtfully curated sake program featuring a diverse selection of premium labels that range from crisp and refreshing to rich and complex, allowing guests to discover the perfect pairing for every sushi dish. The restaurant has also expanded and enhanced its wine list, offering an expertly selected collection of varietals from around the world that pair beautifully with the new sushi menu, hand-cut steaks, and signature entrées. Together, the new beverage program creates an elevated culinary experience where every bite and sip is intentionally crafted to complement one another.

The anniversary celebration marks more than just a year in business — it reflects ZuZu‘s commitment to continually evolving its menu while creating memorable experiences for both Nashville locals and visitors alike.

“Reaching our first anniversary is an incredible milestone, and we’re grateful to every guest who has made ZuZu part of their celebrations over the past year,” said Angelo Ferrante, General Manager of ZuZu Nashville. “Our team is thrilled to debut the new sushi and sake program, along with an expanded wine list. Each day, we’re driven by a passion for creativity, quality and hospitality which cultivates our innovation. These offerings bring a fresh energy to the menu while staying true to the vibrant dining experience our guests have come to expect — an immersive dining experience that celebrates the art of pairing exceptional food and beverages.”

Guests can enjoy the new offerings alongside ZuZu‘s signature cocktails and existing menu favorites, creating an ideal setting for happy hour, date nights, celebrations and group gatherings.

As ZuZu enters its second year, the restaurant remains committed to delivering exceptional hospitality, innovative cuisine and an atmosphere that continues to make it one of Nashville’s must-visit dining destinations.

For more information about ZuZu Nashville, upcoming events, or to make a reservation, visit the restaurant’s website or follow @ExperienceZuZuNashville on social media.
