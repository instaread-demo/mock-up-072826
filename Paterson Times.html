Paterson starts week sunny before showers and storms move in

  • Briefing
  • Weather
July 20, 2026

Sunny and seasonable weather is expected across Paterson and nearby communities on Monday before showers and possible thunderstorms move into the area Tuesday.

Monday will be sunny, with a high near 85. Winds will be calm before becoming southwest around 5 mph in the afternoon. Clouds will increase on Monday night, with a low around 67 and south winds of 3 to 7 mph.

Wet weather returns on Tuesday, when showers become likely and a thunderstorm is possible after 11 a.m. The day will be mostly cloudy, with a high near 83. Southeast winds will be 7 to 11 mph, with gusts as high as 21 mph. The chance of rain is 60 percent, with new rainfall amounts between a quarter and half an inch possible.

Showers and possibly a thunderstorm will continue on Tuesday night, mainly before 2 a.m. The low will be around 70, with southwest winds around 7 mph. The chance of rain rises to 90 percent, and another quarter to half-inch of rain is possible.

A few showers may linger on Wednesday, with thunderstorms also possible after 2 p.m. The day will be partly sunny, with a high near 84 and southwest winds of 8 to 11 mph. The chance of precipitation is 30 percent. Skies are expected to clear Wednesday night, with a low around 62.

Drier weather is expected on Thursday and Friday. Thursday will be sunny, with a high near 81, while Friday will also be sunny, with a high near 83.

The weekend will bring warmer but mainly quiet conditions. Saturday will be mostly sunny, with a high near 86, and Sunday will be mostly sunny, with a high near 88.

Residents should plan for a wet Tuesday commute and be prepared for brief heavy downpours, lightning and ponding on roads during thunderstorms.
