Ramona evacuations ordered with fast-moving Creelman Fire

  • July 22, 2026

Update July 24, 2026 9 a.m. — The fire is 60% contained and has burned 346.7 acres, For updates on evacuation orders and warnings, see the Genasys evacuation map.

Update 6:55 p.m. — An overnight shelter is now open at Olive Pierce Middle School, 1521 Hanson Lane, Ramona.  Small animals are allowed at this shelter.  The temporary shelter at Ramona High School has closed. Large animals can be taken to the Ramona Rodeo grounds.  If you need help to evacuate pets that you can’t reach due to road closures, call California Animal Rescue Rangers Evacuation Team at 760-733-2135

Update 5:38 p.m. — SDG&E has shut off power to over 1,100 customers in Ramona Country Estates for safety due to the fire. Road Closures:  Vista Ramona Road at Old Julian Highway, San Vincente Road at Wildcat Canyon Road.

Update 4:40 p.m.– The fire has grown to 314.8 acres with structure protection underway at the Ramona Country Estates.  The Incident Commander has requested major resources for tomorrow, indicating that an extended attack is anticipated. For the latest evacuation orders and warnings in the fast-moving fire and a map, visit https://protect.genasys.com/en?lat=33.0281&lng=-116.8346&z=12.9.

East County News Service

July 22, 2026 (Ramona) — Firefighters are battling a fast-moving vegetation fire this afternoon in Ramona called the Creelman Fire that grew rapidly and threatened homes, prompting evacuation orders for several neighborhoods as crews mounted an aggressive air and ground attack.

The blaze broke out on the 300 block of Creelman Lane around 3 p.m., according to the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection. The fire had burned 10 acres initially and grew quickly. Within 15 minutes it scorched an estimated 60 to 80 acres, spreading at what Watch Duty described as a “moderate to dangerous rate.”

Structures along Gem Lane were among those threatened, according to Watch Duty.

The cause remains under investigation.

Firefighters requested additional resources and launched an extended attack to slow the fire’s advance while protecting structures in the rural community.

The San Diego County Sheriff’s Office issued mandatory evacuation orders for zones SDC-1168, SDC-1242, SDC-1243, SDC-1244, SDC-1245 and SDC-1247. Evacuation warnings were issued for zones SDC-1166, SDC-1167, SDC-1241, SDC-1246, SDC-1248 and SDC-1249. Officials urged residents who need extra time to leave — including those with livestock or pets — to evacuate before conditions worsen.

A temporary evacuation point was established at Ramona High School, 1401 Hanson Lane, while the Ramona Rodeo grounds at 421 Aqua Lane were designated as a shelter for large animals.

CAL FIRE confirmed a road closure on Vista Ramona Road at Old Julian Highway as crews worked to keep evacuation routes clear. Residents were urged to avoid the area to allow emergency crews to operate safely.

Thick smoke could be seen rising above Ramona as helicopters and fixed-wing aircraft joined engines and hand crews on the ground.

CAL FIRE and the County of San Diego Sheriff’s Office continued to monitor the fire Wednesday afternoon, warning that evacuation areas could change as conditions evolve.

Residents are encouraged to monitor official evacuation information through AlertSanDiego.org and follow CAL FIRE San Diego and the San Diego County Sheriff’s Office for the latest updates as firefighters continue working toward containment.
