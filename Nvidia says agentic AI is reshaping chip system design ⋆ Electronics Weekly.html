Nvidia says agentic AI is reshaping chip system design

Sponsored by

by Caroline Hayes
28th July 2026
in AI, EDA and IP, News, Products, Software
0

Agentic engineering is reshaping chip and system design and Nvidia is putting itself at the core of this movement with a concerted effort to deliver the tools and libraries designers will need.

In addition to updates on how ‘the big three’ of EDA, Cadence, Siemens and Synopsys, have integrated Nvidia products into their toolkits, Timothy Costa, Nvidia’s vice president and general manager for industrial and computational engineering, used his welcome keynote to announce the addition of PhysicsNeMo and Cuda-X librairies to the company’s Agent Toolkit.

The PhysicsNeMo libraries have been re-architected and updates to the Cuda-X libraries, are both designed to enable software developers to build autonomous AI engineers with AI physics skills, accelerated solvers and quantum chemistry capabilities.

The focus of acceleration of core tools is the application of AI as a surrogate technology to dramatically speed design, said Costa. “Engineering has reached an inflection point. AI can now work with tools of physics, simulation and design,” he said.

Physics NeMo is now a set of agent-friendly libraries and included in the Nvidia Agent Toolkit. It provides AI physics skills for training and deploying models. Updates to the Cuda-X libraries include accelerated solvers and quantum chemistry capabilities for use in agentic engineering workflows.

Developers can use the Agent Toolkit to build agentic engineers “that reason using physics, run complex simulations and generate high-fidelity data to become a new engine for innovation in chip and system design,” explained Costa.

In additon to PhysicsNeMo libraries to help agents train and deploy customisable AI physics models, there are also new libraries for iterative and direct sparse solvers. The Cuda Iterative Sparse Solvers (cuISS) library accelerates large sparse linear systems in physics-based and engineering simulations on GPUs. Its composable solvers and pre-conditioners can be used to build scalable, production simulation engines for agentic engineering workflows.

CuDSS (Cuda Direct Sparse Solvers) accelerates large, complex sparse linear systems for EDA and scientific simulation in critical workloads like device, circuit and system simulations. They are scalable to multi-GPU and multi-node deployments in production environments.

There is also cuEST (Cuda Electronic Structure Theory) with quantum chemistry simulations scaled for device use. Its role is to enable density functional theory (DFT) and post-DFT methods to be integrated into production workflows at scale for GPUs.

In the realm of open models, Nvidia’s Nemotron 3 Ultra open model provides agentic register-transfer level coding with the ACE-RTL agent from Nvidia Research. It targets enterprises building customisable AI agents for chip design and verification.

Itis compatible with Cadence’s harness; Synopsys’ autonomous, long-running agents for design verification and analogue and mixed-signal workflows; and Siemens’ Questa One smart verification agentic toolkit and the AI community platformu, Hugging Face.
