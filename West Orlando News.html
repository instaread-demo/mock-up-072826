Florida Man Claimed $5 Million Prize from Scratch-Off Game

By Staff
July 20, 2026

Sponsored by

Instaread

A Florida man claimed a $5 million prize from a scratch-off game.




The Florida Lottery recently announced that Paul Cich, of DeFuniak Springs, claimed a $5 million top prize from the 200X The Cash scratch-off game.

The North Florida man chose to receive his winnings as a one-time, lump-sum payment of $2,779,400.

The Walton County winner purchased his winning ticket from Tom Thumb, located at 1966 U.S. 90 W. in DeFuniak Springs.

He claimed the top prize at lottery headquarters in Tallahassee.

200X The Cash is a $20 scratch-off game offering players the chance to win a top prize of $5,000,000. There is one top prize remaining according to the Florida Lottery. Designed for players who love big multipliers and big excitement, the Florida Lottery says this powerful $20 ticket delivers thrilling play and massive prize potential.

With overall odds of 1-in-2.96, this high-value game features more than 6.9 million winning tickets and over $313 million in total cash prizes.

Scratch-off games are an important part of the Lottery’s portfolio of games, comprising approximately 74 percent of ticket sales in fiscal year 2023-2024. Additionally, since inception, scratch-off games have awarded more than $70.8 billion in prizes, created 2,475 millionaires, and generated more than $20.98 billion for the state’s Educational Enhancement Trust Fund (EETF).

The Florida Lottery is responsible for contributing more than $49 billion to enhance education and sending more than 983,000 students to college through the Bright Futures Scholarship Program. The Florida Lottery reinvests 99 percent of its revenue into Florida’s economy through prize payouts, commissions to more than 13,550 Lottery retailers, and transfers to education. Since 1988, Florida Lottery games have paid over $104.8 billion in prizes and created more than 4,475 millionaires.
